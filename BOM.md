# Provisional BOM

This is a planning-stage shortlist, not a procurement release.

## COTS hardware families in scope

## Cable entry

- icotek `KDR-BES-U` brush strip for custom long slot use
- icotek `KEL-BES-S` split brush plate family
- McMaster wraparound cable-entry panels for serviceable split-entry alternatives

## Panel retention fallback

- Southco captive screw families such as `47/4C`, `51/53/58`, and `F5`
- McMaster quarter-turn captive panel fasteners
- McMaster or PEM-compatible captive nuts / studs if a hybrid stud-and-knob closure becomes simpler than latch reuse

## Structural hardware

- PEM self-clinching nuts or studs for cassette-to-shell attachment
- rivnuts or plusnuts only if access prevents proper clinch hardware
- steel or aluminum angle reinforcement if PSU-adjacent loads exceed shell stiffness

## Prototype materials

- cheap filament for fit coupons
- cardboard, foam board, or coroplast for volume studies
- 1.0-1.5 mm sheet for fast alpha flat tests

## Selection notes

- Do not buy latch or captive hardware until the top-retention strategy is selected.
- Do not buy cable-entry hardware until the cable bundle is measured as built.
- If the PSU rides on the enclosure, structural hardware needs its own load-path review rather than being treated as panel trim.
