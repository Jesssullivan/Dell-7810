# CAD Handoff Checklist

This checklist prevents premature modeling. Each task below has a minimum measurement threshold.

## Coupon 1: lower rail

Required measurements:

- `IF-003`
- `IF-004F`
- `IF-004M`
- `IF-004R`
- `IF-005F`
- `IF-005M`
- `IF-005R`
- `IF-006`
- `IF-007`

Required evidence:

- one full-length lower rail photo
- at least one close-up with scale at front or center
- one note on whether the profile changes front to rear

Allowed uncertainty:

- small local stamped irregularities

Not allowed:

- guessed groove width
- guessed lip depth

## Coupon 2: top latch

Required measurements:

- `IF-008`
- `IF-009`
- `IF-010`
- `IF-011`
- `IF-012`
- `IF-015`

Required evidence:

- one overall latch-location photo
- one close-up of the mating feature
- one photo or sketch that explains latch motion

Not allowed:

- assuming the latch is centered by symmetry
- inferring the hook width from a blurry photo

## Coupon 3: rear edge / rear top corner

Required measurements:

- `IF-013`
- `IF-014`

Required evidence:

- one rear-corner photo with scale
- one card or paper transfer if the shape is not easy to dimension numerically

Not allowed:

- reducing a compound corner to a single rectangle without evidence

## Coupon 4: cable opening test plate

Required measurements:

- `CBL-001`
- `CBL-002`
- `CBL-003`
- `CBL-004`
- `CBL-005`

Required evidence:

- one photo of the real compressed bundle
- one photo of the largest terminated connector cluster
- one photo of the preferred panel zone marked in tape

Not allowed:

- sizing the opening from nominal wire gauges

## P1 shell blank

Required measurements:

- all critical lower-rail features
- all critical top-retention features
- rear corner location evidence
- `GPU-002`
- `PATH-001`
- `PATH-002`
- `PATH-003`

Required decision:

- whether P1 is using the OEM latch or captive fasteners at the top

## P2 utility cassette blank

Required measurements:

- cable bundle measurements
- preferred cable-opening zone
- PSU target relationship if the cassette also acts as a light shelf or alignment fence

Required decision:

- brush slot vs split-entry family for the first prototype

## Go / no-go rule

If an interface has not been measured cleanly enough to support a coupon, do not roll it into the main shell. Print a smaller coupon or re-measure it.
